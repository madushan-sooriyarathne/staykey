Owner app screens

All 37 V1 screens of the iOS and Android owner app, excluding onboarding. Styled on the brand reference: zinc neutrals and hairline borders, with Ember marking direct bookings and anything that needs the owner, and Magenta Spark marking wins and live status.

Main tabs

The five tabs an owner moves between all day. Today opens by default after sign-in.

Booking screens

Everything that happens to a single booking, from a WhatsApp entry to a cancellation.

Calendar sheets

Quick actions that open after the owner selects a date range on the calendar.

Property settings

Reached from a property's overview. Each screen saves on its own, so an owner can fix one thing while on the move.

More

Account, team and support screens, reached from the More tab.

Caretaker view

The same app with a restricted role: Today, Calendar and booking details without prices, payments or settings.

Sample data throughout. Guest names, prices and the direct.lk address are placeholders until the product name and domain are chosen.
