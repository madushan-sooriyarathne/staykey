Onboarding screens

16 screens that take an owner from install to a live booking page in about 8 minutes, plus the path for invited team members. Every step autosaves, and anything not needed to take a first booking moves to a checklist after launch.

16 screens 4 stages Ember: progress and action Magenta Spark: wins and live
Stage 1: You Stage 2: Your property Stage 3: Getting paid Stage 4: Go live After setup Team invites

Stage 1: You

A phone number is the whole account. The same number receives WhatsApp booking alerts later.

Stage 2: Your property

Enough for a guest to picture the stay. Amenities, description and extra photos join the checklist.

Stage 3: Getting paid

Methods that work the same day, with deposit and cancellation rules prefilled from sensible defaults.

Stage 4: Go live

A safety check for owners already on OTAs, a final review, then the page goes live and the owner can share it.

After setup

Everything onboarding skipped, ordered by impact and kept on Today until it's done or dismissed.

Team invites

Managers and caretakers arrive from a WhatsApp or SMS invite link, verify the same way as owners, then land here. Property setup is skipped.

Sample data throughout. Names, prices, bank details and the direct.lk address are placeholders until the product name and domain are chosen.
